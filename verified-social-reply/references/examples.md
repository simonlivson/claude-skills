These are format samples only. Do not copy the facts into a real reply.

## Good: a short reply after a check

Post (sample): "Our paper reports a 12.4% improvement on the public benchmark. Link: example.org/paper."
The paper, opened from that link, states 12.4% on that benchmark.

```
Reply (draft):
12.4% on the public benchmark is a clear result. Did you also report the variance across runs?

Checked:
- 12.4% improvement on the public benchmark : the paper linked in the post, results section

Skip?: no
```
